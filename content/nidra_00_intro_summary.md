# Programme introduction — summary

*How to travel the journey.*

- **The aim** — learn to run Yoga Nidra on yourself, from memory. The recordings are only scaffolding.
- **Grounded** in the ancient sources, not a modern method.
- **Two kinds of recording:** *orientation teachings* (just listen) and *practice modules*.
- **Each practice module** has three parts — a *teaching* (hear once), a short *daily practice*, and a longer *extended practice* — plus a cue card.
- **Move on by feel, not the calendar** — when a practice feels natural and you could roughly do it from memory (a few days to a week each).
- **Nothing is left behind** — each element folds into the next, until the whole practice is yours.
