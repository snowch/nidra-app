A closing word.

<break time="2.0s" />

You have come to the end of the programme. But not, really, to the end of the practice. For now, the practice is yours.

<break time="1.5s" />

Remember what all of this has been for. Not simply to relax, though you will. Not simply to sleep better, though you may. The ancient texts point to something further.

<break time="1.5s" />

Beneath the waking mind, beneath the dreaming mind, beneath even deep sleep, they say there is an awareness that never sleeps. The silent witness. Turiya. The one you have been quietly turning towards, all along.

<break time="1.5s" />

Yoga Nidra is a doorway to that. Each time you lie down, withdraw, and rest as the one who is aware, you come a little closer to knowing, directly, what you most deeply are.

<break time="1.5s" />

So keep your resolve. Keep the practice. Let it be short and frequent, or long and rare, as your life allows. And let it carry you, gently, inward.

<break time="1.5s" />

The scaffolding has done its work. The voice falls quiet now.

<break time="1.5s" />

What remains, is you.
