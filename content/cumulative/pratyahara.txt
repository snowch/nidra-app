Now, begin to withdraw the senses, gently, one by one...

<break time="4.0s" />

First, hearing... let the sounds around you come, and go... near sounds, and far... you need not follow them... let them arrive, and pass, like birds crossing the sky...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now, touch... feel the body resting... its weight... the warmth, or the coolness, of the air... let each sensation simply be, without reaching towards it...

<break time="8.0s" />

Notice how the senses reach outward, towards the world... and now, gently, let them turn, and begin to flow inward... like a tortoise, drawing its limbs beneath its shell...

<break time="8.0s" />

And rest, here, turned inward... needing nothing, from the world outside...
