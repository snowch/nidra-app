Bring your attention to the breath... and do not change it... simply let the body breathe itself, and watch...

<break time="5.0s" />

Feel the breath flowing in... and flowing out... perhaps the gentle rise, and fall, of the belly... or the cool air, at the nostrils...

<break time="6.0s" />

To steady the mind, count the breaths, backwards, from eleven... breathing in, and out, eleven... in, and out, ten... and so on, slowly, in your own time. If you lose the count, simply begin again...

<break time="100.0s" />

Let the counting go... and simply rest, with the breath...

<break time="5.0s" />

And notice, if you can, the small stillness at the end of the out-breath... before the next breath begins...

<break time="6.0s" />

Perhaps there is a moment of quiet there...

<break time="8.0s" />

Rest, gently, in that stillness... and let each new breath arrive, on its own...
