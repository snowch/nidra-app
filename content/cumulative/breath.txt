Now bring your attention to the breath... and do not change it... simply let the body breathe itself, and watch...

<break time="5.0s" />

Feel the breath flowing in... and flowing out... perhaps the gentle rise, and fall, of the belly... or the cool air, at the nostrils...

<break time="6.0s" />

Now, to steady the mind, begin to count the breaths, backwards, from twenty-seven... Breathing in, and out, twenty-seven... in, and out, twenty-six... and on... If you lose the count, simply begin again... Continue now, in your own time...

<break time="15.0s" />

Now let the counting go... and simply rest, with the breath...

<break time="5.0s" />

And notice the small pause... at the end of each out-breath... before the next breath begins... In that gap, the breath is still... and the mind, for a moment, is still, too...

<break time="8.0s" />

Rest, gently, in that quiet gap... and let each new breath come, on its own...
