Through this whole practice, something has been quietly aware... aware of the body... of the breath... of the images, and the silence... Now, we turn towards that awareness, itself...

<break time="6.0s" />

Let everything be, just as it is... the sounds... the sensations... the thoughts, drifting by... You do not need to change any of it...

<break time="6.0s" />

And gently, ask... who is aware of all this?... Not with words, or thinking... but simply, turn towards the one who is aware...

<break time="8.0s" />

You are not the sounds... you are the one who hears them... You are not the thoughts... you are the one who watches them, come, and go...

<break time="8.0s" />

Rest, now, as that awareness... the silent witness... aware, and at peace... whole, and needing nothing...

<break time="10.0s" />

If it helps, let the sound of AUM arise, silently, within you... Ahh... Ooo... Mmm... and then, rest, in the silence that follows the sound... That silence is your own, deepest nature... the awareness, that is always here...

<break time="12.0s" />

Rest, as awareness itself...
