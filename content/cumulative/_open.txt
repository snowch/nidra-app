Make yourself comfortable, lying down on your back...

<break time="3.0s" />

Let the legs fall open... the arms resting a little away from the body... the palms turned upward...

<break time="4.0s" />

Allow the eyes to gently close...

<break time="4.0s" />

Allow the body to become as still as is comfortable. There is no need to move. And if you do need to move, move slowly, and return to stillness...

<break time="5.0s" />

There is nothing to do now... nowhere to be... nothing to achieve...

<break time="5.0s" />

Feel the whole weight of the body, held by the floor beneath you... let it grow heavy... settling down, as if sinking, just a little, into the ground...

<break time="6.0s" />

Make the quiet promise, to yourself, to remain aware. The body may become very still... perhaps even as though it were sleeping... while awareness remains, quietly present...
