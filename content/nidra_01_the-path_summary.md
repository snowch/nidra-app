# The path — summary

*Where Yoga Nidra sits.*

- The yogic path has **eight steps**, in two halves.
- **The first four face outward** — how you live, the steadiness of the body, the calming of the breath. Preparation.
- **The fifth is the turning point** — attention stops flowing outward and begins to flow inward. **This is where Yoga Nidra begins.**
- **Beyond it**, the final steps go deeper still — toward deep concentration and stillness.
- Nidra itself grew from an older, tantric root; the eight-step map only shows where it sits: **at the doorway between the outer world and the inner one.**
