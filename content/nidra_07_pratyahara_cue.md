# M3 · Pratyahara — cue card

*Withdraw the senses. Not shutting out — no longer reaching towards.*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana (see Setting up); Stillness (M1); a saṅkalpa chosen (M2).

1. **Settle** → **Resolve** (×3)
2. **Sounds** — come and go, don't follow
3. **Sensation** — let it be, don't reach
4. **Turn inward** — senses quiet
5. **Rest** withdrawn → **Resolve** → **Return**

> **Ready to move on:** Senses quiet on their own; sounds and sensations come and go without pulling you out.
