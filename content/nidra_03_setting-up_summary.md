# Setting up — summary

*Preparing to practise.*

- **Lie on your back** in shavasana — legs open, arms a little away, palms up, completely still.
- **Keep warm** — the body cools; use a blanket. Quiet, dimly lit room; cover the eyes if it helps.
- **Empty or near-empty stomach.** Any time of day; early morning or early evening are especially still.
- **If you fall asleep, that's not failure** — begin again where you can. Over time you learn to rest on the threshold: relaxed, but aware.
- If sleep keeps winning, slightly raise the head, or keep the practice shorter.
