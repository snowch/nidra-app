# The depths — summary

*How deep the journey reaches.*

You are described as **five nested layers**, each subtler than the last:

1. the **physical body**
2. **breath and energy**
3. the **mind** — thoughts, sensations, feelings
4. a quiet **knowing** that watches the mind
5. a still **centre** of deep, causeless peace

- Yoga Nidra travels slowly inward, from the body toward the centre.
- You don't force it — as the outer layers grow still, the subtler ones reveal themselves.
- **"Deeper" means further inward — not down into sleep.**
