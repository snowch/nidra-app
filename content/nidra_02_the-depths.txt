Now we turn to the depths.

<break time="2.0s" />

The path showed the journey as steps, going forward. The ancient texts offer a second map. A journey inward, through the layers of what you are.

<break time="1.5s" />

The old teaching, from the Upanishads, describes you as a series of five layers. Like vessels, one held inside the next. Each one subtler than the last.

<break time="1.5s" />

The outermost layer is the physical body. The body you will make still.

<break time="1.5s" />

Within it, a subtler layer. The layer of breath, and of energy. You touch this one when you turn your attention to the breath.

<break time="1.5s" />

Deeper still, the layer of the mind. Thoughts, sensations, and feelings. The endless movement of the surface mind.

<break time="1.5s" />

Subtler again, a quiet layer of knowing. The awareness that watches the mind, rather than being caught up in it.

<break time="1.5s" />

And at the very centre, a layer of deep, and causeless, peace. A stillness that has nothing to do with anything happening.

<break time="2.0s" />

Yoga Nidra is a slow journey through these layers. From the body... to the breath... through the moving mind... to the one who watches... and toward that quiet centre.

<break time="1.5s" />

You do not have to force your way through. As the outer layers grow still, the subtler ones simply reveal themselves.

<break time="1.5s" />

So when you hear the word deeper, in this programme, this is what it means. Not further down, into sleep. But further inward, through the layers, toward the stillness at your centre.

<break time="2.0s" />

Keep this map beside the first. The path shows where you are going. And the layers show how deep it reaches.
