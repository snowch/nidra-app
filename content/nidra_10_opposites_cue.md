# M6 · Pairs of opposites — cue card

*Feel each fully; be moved by neither. Equanimity (Gita).*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana; Stillness (M1); your saṅkalpa (M2).

1. **Settle** → **Resolve** (×3)
2. **Heavy** ↔ **Light**
3. **Warm** ↔ **Cool**
4. Feel each, release, meet its opposite
5. **Resolve** → **Return**

> **Ready to move on:** Each sensation felt fully, then released for its opposite; even, and unmoved.
