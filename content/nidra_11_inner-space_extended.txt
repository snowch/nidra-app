Settle down, and become still...

<break time="4.0s" />

Repeat your resolve, silently, three times...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now bring your awareness to the dark space, behind the closed eyes... the inner sky... chidakasha... the space, in which everything is seen...

<break time="6.0s" />

Simply rest your gaze there, softly... looking into the darkness... without straining... without searching for anything...

<break time="8.0s" />

Whatever appears in the space... a faint colour... a drifting shape... a memory, or an image... let it arise, and let it dissolve... You are not the one who makes them... you are the space, in which they come, and go...

<break time="10.0s" />

If the mind is restless, rest your attention on a single point of light... a small, steady flame... in the centre of the darkness... and let it gather your attention, gently, into one place...

<break time="10.0s" />

And now, let even the point of light dissolve... and rest, simply, in the space itself... vast... dark... silent... and still...

<break time="10.0s" />

The inner space asks nothing of you... it only holds, whatever appears... as the sky holds the passing clouds...

<break time="10.0s" />

Rest, in the space...

<break time="10.0s" />

And slowly, let the awareness return... to the body... and the breath...

<break time="4.0s" />

repeat your resolve, once more...

<break time="5.0s" />

and gently open your eyes.
