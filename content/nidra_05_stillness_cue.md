# M1 · Stillness — cue card

*Ground of the practice: be still, stay awake at the edge of sleep.*

**Why —** Establishes the still, alert threshold that every later stage rests on.

> **Before you begin:** Orientation teachings heard; lying in shavasana.

1. **Lie down** — shavasana, palms up, completely still
2. **The promise** — *"I will remain aware"*
3. **Rest** — body heavy, awareness awake, at the edge
4. **Return** — breath, gently up

> **If it's not working —** Fidgeting, or drifting into sleep. Let small itches pass unscratched if you can; if you must move, move slowly and settle again. If you keep falling asleep, practise a little more upright, or first thing rather than last.

> **Going deeper —** You are not making stillness happen — you are noticing the stillness already there, beneath the body's small movements and the breath.

> **Ready to move on:** Still within a breath; rest at the edge (not asleep) for the whole practice; find it unaided.
