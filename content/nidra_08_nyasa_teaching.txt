Module four. Nyasa.

<break time="2.0s" />

Nyasa is an ancient tantric practice. To place awareness, deliberately, upon each part of the body, one after another.

<break time="1.5s" />

It is the heart of Yoga Nidra. A rotation of consciousness, moving through the body in a set order, touching each part with a light, effortless attention.

<break time="1.5s" />

You do not tense the part, or move it, or even picture it. You simply hear its name, and let your awareness rest there for a moment. Then move on. The right hand. The right arm. And so through the whole body.

<break time="1.5s" />

This rotation does two things. It relaxes the body, very deeply. And it gathers the restless mind into a single, flowing thread of attention. Too occupied, now, to wander.

<break time="1.5s" />

Traditionally, the rotation begins on the right side of the body, moves to the left, then to the back, the front, and finally the head. A river of awareness, flowing through you.

<break time="1.5s" />

In the practice, simply follow the voice. Let each part grow bright, softly, as it is named. Then soften, as you move on.
