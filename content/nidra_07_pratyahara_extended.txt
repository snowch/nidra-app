Settle down, and let the body become still...

<break time="4.0s" />

Repeat your resolve, silently, three times...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now we begin to withdraw the senses, gently, one by one...

<break time="4.0s" />

First, hearing... Let the sounds around you come, and go... near sounds... and far sounds... You do not need to name them, or follow them... let them arrive, and pass, like birds crossing the sky...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now, touch... Feel the body resting... the weight of it... the warmth, or the coolness, of the air... And let each sensation simply be, without reaching towards it...

<break time="8.0s" />

Notice how the senses reach outward, towards the world... and now, gently, let them turn... and begin to flow inward...

<break time="6.0s" />

You are not shutting the world out... the sounds are still there... the sensations are still there... you are simply, no longer reaching towards them...

<break time="8.0s" />

Like a tortoise, drawing its limbs beneath its shell... let the senses draw quietly inward...

<break time="8.0s" />

And rest, here, in the quiet that remains... turned inward... needing nothing, from the world outside...

<break time="10.0s" />

Rest...

<break time="8.0s" />

And slowly, let the senses open again... hearing the sounds of the room... feeling the body...

<break time="4.0s" />

repeat your resolve, once more...

<break time="5.0s" />

and gently return, opening your eyes.
