# M9 · The return — cue card

*Come back slowly. Resolve first, then externalise.*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana; Stillness (M1); your saṅkalpa (M2).

1. **Resolve** — one final time, ×3 (mind still deep)
2. **Breath** — natural breath
3. **Body** — heavy, still
4. **Room** — sounds, space
5. **Move** — fingers, toes, stretch
6. **Eyes open** — when fully present

> **Ready to move on:** You return slowly and completely on your own — resolve, breath, body, room, movement — fully present.
