# M8 · The return — cue card

*Come back slowly, and completely.*

**Why —** Brings you back from the depths, gently, and unhurried.

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana (see Setting up); Stillness (M1).

1. **Breath** — natural breath
2. **Body** — heavy, still
3. **Room** — sounds, space
4. **Move** — fingers, toes, stretch
5. **Eyes open** — when fully present

> **If it's not working —** Don't rush up — grogginess comes from returning too fast. If you feel heavy or spaced, take longer over the breath and the small movements before you sit.

> **Going deeper —** Carry the witness with you — the stillness is not left behind on the floor; it is the ground you stand up into.

> **Ready to move on:** You return slowly and completely on your own — breath, body, room, movement — fully present.
