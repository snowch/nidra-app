Module eight. The witness.

<break time="2.0s" />

Through all the stages so far, something has been quietly present. The one who is aware. The witness, behind every sound, every sensation, every image.

<break time="1.5s" />

The Mandukya Upanishad describes four states. Waking. Dreaming. Deep sleep. And turiya, the fourth. Not a state you enter, but the pure awareness present within all the others. The silent witness of everything that changes.

<break time="1.5s" />

In inner space, you watched what arises. Now, you turn towards the one who watches. You rest, not as the body, not as the breath, not as the passing thoughts. But as awareness itself. Aware, and at peace, and needing nothing.

<break time="1.5s" />

The Mandukya gives a key to this. The sound of AUM. Ah, the waking state. Oo, the dream. Mm, deep sleep. And the silence that follows the sound. That silence is turiya. The awareness that remains, when all sound has ceased.

<break time="1.5s" />

In the practice, you may sound AUM, silently, or aloud. And then rest in the silence that follows. Resting, simply, as the one who is aware.


<break time="1.5s" />

How will you know when you are ready to move on? When you can turn, gently, from the things you are aware of... to the one who is aware... and rest there, as awareness itself, even for a few moments, without effort, and without words.

<break time="1.5s" />

There is no hurry. Stay with this, until it feels like your own.
