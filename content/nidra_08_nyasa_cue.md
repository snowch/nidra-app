# M4 · Nyasa — cue card

*Rotate awareness through the body. Hear the part, rest, move on. No moving.*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana (see Setting up); Stillness (M1); your saṅkalpa (M2).

1. **Settle** → **Resolve** (×3)
2. **Rotate** — right side → left side → back → front → head
3. **Whole body at once** — still
4. **Resolve** → **Return**

> **Ready to move on:** Awareness flows through the body on its own, without searching or losing your place.
