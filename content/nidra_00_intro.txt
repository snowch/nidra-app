Welcome to this practice.

<break time="2.0s" />

Before the first module, a few words about the journey you are beginning, and how to travel it.

<break time="2.0s" />

First, what this programme is for.

<break time="1.2s" />

The aim is simple, and a little unusual. Not to give you a recording to be played each night, but to teach you to perform Yoga Nidra on yourself, from memory, unaided.

<break time="1.5s" />

Recordings like this one are only the scaffolding. Over time, the scaffolding comes down, and the practice remains yours.

<break time="1.5s" />

Everything here is drawn from the ancient sources. The Upanishads, the Yoga Sutras of Patanjali, and the tantric texts. Not from a modern method. You will learn each element from the tradition it came from.

<break time="1.5s" />

By the end, you will be able to lie down in stillness, withdraw the senses, move awareness through the body and the breath, rest as the silent witness, and return. All on your own.

<break time="2.0s" />

Now, how to follow it.

<break time="1.2s" />

The journey unfolds in two kinds of recording.

<break time="1.5s" />

A few are orientation teachings, like this one, and the two that follow. They set the map, and you simply listen.

<break time="1.5s" />

The rest are practice modules. Each of these has three parts. First, a short teaching, to understand what you are about to practise, and where it comes from. Listen to it once.

<break time="1.2s" />

Second, a short practice, only a few minutes long. This is the one you return to, again and again. Ideally, every day.

<break time="1.2s" />

And third, a longer practice, for when time allows, that gathers everything you have learned so far into one deepening flow.

<break time="1.5s" />

Do the short practice often. Let the longer one be a gift you give yourself when you can.

<break time="2.0s" />

And finally, when to move on.

<break time="1.2s" />

Not by the calendar, but by feel.

<break time="1.5s" />

Stay with each module until its practice feels natural. Until you can begin it, and settle into it, without leaning on every word. Until you could, roughly, do it from memory.

<break time="1.5s" />

For most, that is a few days to a week with each module. The deeper practices may ask for longer. There is no prize for hurrying. In this tradition, depth comes from repetition, not from moving quickly.

<break time="1.5s" />

And as you go, you do not leave the earlier modules behind. Each new element folds into the ones before it. Until, in the final module, they become a single, continuous practice, performed entirely by you.

<break time="2.0s" />

That is the whole arc. Withdraw... deepen... witness... and return.

<break time="1.5s" />

When you are ready, begin with Module One. Stillness.
