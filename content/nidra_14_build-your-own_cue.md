# M10 · The whole practice — cue card

*The complete Yoga Nidra, from memory. Ten steps.*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana; all elements learned (M1–M9).

1. **Stillness** — lie down, still, awake
2. **Resolve** — sankalpa ×3
3. **Withdraw** — senses (pratyahara)
4. **Rotate** — through the body (nyasa)
5. **Breathe** — breath & the gap
6. **Balance** — pairs of opposites
7. **Space** — inner space (chidakasha)
8. **Witness** — rest as awareness (turiya)
9. **Resolve again** — ×3, mind deep
10. **Return** — breath, body, room, move, eyes open

> *Stillness · Resolve · Withdraw · Rotate · Breathe · Balance · Space · Witness · Resolve · Return*

> **Ready to move on:** You move through the whole practice from memory, at your own pace, without the recording.
