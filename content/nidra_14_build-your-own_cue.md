# M9 · The whole practice — cue card

*The whole flow, from memory. Eight steps.*

**Why —** The whole flow, run from memory — the aim of the programme. (The resolve is added last, once this is effortless — see Sankalpa, M10.)

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana (see Setting up); all elements learned (M1–M8).

1. **Stillness** — lie down, still, awake
2. **Withdraw** — senses (pratyahara)
3. **Rotate** — through the body (nyasa)
4. **Breathe** — breath & the gap
5. **Balance** — pairs of opposites
6. **Space** — inner space (chidakasha)
7. **Witness** — rest as awareness (turiya)
8. **Return** — breath, body, room, move, eyes open

> *Stillness · Withdraw · Rotate · Breathe · Balance · Space · Witness · Return*

> **If it's not working —** If you lose the thread, don't restart — pick up at the next stage from memory; the order matters more than completeness. Keep a light hand on the timing.

> **Going deeper —** The recording is scaffolding — the practice is already yours. The aim is not to recall the steps, but to move as awareness through them.

> **Ready to move on:** You move through the whole practice from memory, at your own pace, without the recording.
