Module nine. The return.

<break time="2.0s" />

Every journey inward must be completed by a gentle journey back. The return, or externalisation, is not an afterthought. It is part of the practice, and it is done with care.

<break time="1.5s" />

Coming back too quickly, from so deep a stillness, can leave you dazed. So we return slowly, in stages, retracing our steps.

<break time="1.5s" />

First, we plant the resolve, the sankalpa, one final time, while the mind is still deep, and receptive. This is the most powerful moment for it.

<break time="1.5s" />

Then we become aware, once more, of the breath. Of the body, lying heavy, and still. Of the sounds in the room. Of the space where we are lying.

<break time="1.5s" />

Then we begin to make small movements. The fingers. The toes. A gentle stretch. And only when we feel fully present, and awake, do we let the eyes open.

<break time="1.5s" />

Take a moment, then, before you rise. And carry the stillness with you, gently, into whatever comes next.

<break time="1.5s" />

In the practice, you will learn this unhurried return.
