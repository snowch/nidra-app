Module two. Suncalpa.

<break time="2.0s" />

Suncalpa means resolve. A short, heartfelt intention, planted in the depths of the mind.

<break time="1.5s" />

The ancient Chandogya Upanishad teaches: as is a person's deep resolve, so they become. What we hold, quietly and repeatedly, at the root of the mind, slowly shapes who we are.

<break time="1.5s" />

In Yoga Nidra, the suncalpa is planted when the mind is most open. Near the beginning, and again near the end, of each practice.

<break time="1.5s" />

Choosing your suncalpa matters. Let it be one single resolve, not many. Let it be short, and clear. Let it be positive, and stated as though it were already true. Not, I will become peaceful. But, I am at peace. Not a wish for the future, but a truth you are planting now.

<break time="1.5s" />

Choose one that speaks to something deep in you, and then keep it. The same resolve, unchanged, for many months. Even years. Its power comes not from novelty, but from repetition.

<break time="1.5s" />

And if nothing comes at first, do not let the choosing hold you back. You may begin with a simple resolve of being. Such as, I am at peace, just as I am. Plant that, and let a truer one rise to meet you, in its own time.

<break time="1.5s" />

Take your time to find it. And once you have it, it will open, and close, every practice from here on.

<break time="1.5s" />

In the short practice, you will plant it, for the first time.


<break time="1.5s" />

How will you know when you are ready to move on? When you have found a single resolve that feels true to you... and you can bring it to mind, and repeat it, with quiet feeling, without hunting for the words. Once your suncalpa is chosen, and beginning to feel like your own, you are ready to carry it into everything that follows.

<break time="1.5s" />

There is no hurry. Stay with this, until it feels like your own.
