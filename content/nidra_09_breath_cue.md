# M5 · Breath & the gap — cue card

*Let the breath breathe itself. Rest in the still gap.*

> **Before you begin:** Lying in shavasana; Stillness (M1); your saṅkalpa (M2).

1. **Settle** → **Resolve** (×3)
2. **Watch** — breath in… breath out (don't control)
3. **The gap** — the pause after the out-breath; rest there
4. **Resolve** → **Return**

> **Ready to move on:** The breath breathes itself; you rest in the gap without forcing it.
