Module ten. The whole practice, unaided.

<break time="2.0s" />

You have learned each stage on its own. Now they join, into one continuous flow, performed by you.

<break time="1.5s" />

This is the shape of a complete Yoga Nidra, and a thread to remember it by. First, stillness. You lie down, and become still. Then, the resolve. You plant your sankalpa.

<break time="1.5s" />

Then the journey inward. Withdrawing the senses. Rotating awareness through the body. Following the breath, and its quiet gap. Feeling the pairs of opposites. Resting in inner space. And turning to the witness. The one who is aware.

<break time="1.5s" />

Then the return. You plant the resolve, once more, and slowly come back. To the breath. The body. The room. And you open your eyes.

<break time="1.5s" />

Hold the thread. Stillness. Resolve. Withdraw. Rotate. Breathe. Balance. Space. Witness. Resolve again. And return.

<break time="1.5s" />

The cue card will help you learn it by heart. And the full practice that follows will guide you through the whole, from beginning to end. So that soon, you can lead yourself.

<break time="1.5s" />

This is what the whole journey has been for. In the end, not to need the voice at all.
