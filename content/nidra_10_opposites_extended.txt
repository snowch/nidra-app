Repeat your resolve, silently, three times...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now we will feel pairs of opposites... calling each one up fully... and being moved by neither...

<break time="4.0s" />

Begin with heaviness... Feel the whole body becoming heavy... heavy as stone... sinking down, held by the earth... every part of you, growing heavier...

<break time="8.0s" />

And now, release it... and feel the body becoming light... light as a feather... light as air... as if it could rise, and gently float...

<break time="8.0s" />

Now, warmth... Feel a gentle warmth, spreading through the body... from the belly, outward... a soft, glowing heat, filling you...

<break time="8.0s" />

And now, coolness... Feel a cool, clear air... cool upon the skin... a fresh, cool stillness, moving through the body...

<break time="8.0s" />

Feel each one, fully... and notice how you are the one who feels them all... unmoved... steady... resting at the centre...

<break time="8.0s" />

Heavy... and light... warm... and cool... the opposites, arising, and passing... and you, resting, still, in the middle of them all...

<break time="10.0s" />

Let all sensation settle, now, into an even, and quiet, stillness...

<break time="8.0s" />

And when you are ready... repeat your resolve, once more...

<break time="5.0s" />

move gently... and let your eyes open.
