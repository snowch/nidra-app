Settle down, and become still...

<break time="4.0s" />

Repeat your resolve, silently, three times...

<break time="8.0s" />

We will feel pairs of opposites... calling each one up fully... and being moved by neither...

<break time="4.0s" />

Begin with heaviness... Feel the whole body becoming heavy... very heavy... heavy as stone... sinking down, into the surface beneath you...

<break time="10.0s" />

And now, release it... and feel the body becoming light... very light... light as a feather... as if it had almost no weight at all...

<break time="10.0s" />

Heavy... and light...

<break time="10.0s" />

Now, warmth... a gentle warmth, spreading through the body... glowing, from the belly, outward... warmth, throughout the whole body...

<break time="10.0s" />

And now, coolness... a cool, clear air... a gentle coolness, throughout the body...

<break time="10.0s" />

Warmth... and coolness...

<break time="10.0s" />

Bring to mind, now, a feeling of something pleasant... without needing to find a particular memory... simply allow the feeling of pleasantness to arise...

<break time="10.0s" />

And now, allow the feeling of something unpleasant to arise... nothing overwhelming... simply notice the quality of unpleasantness...

<break time="10.0s" />

Pleasant... and unpleasant...

<break time="10.0s" />

And now, let both be present, without choosing between them... neither grasping... neither resisting... simply aware...

<break time="10.0s" />

Be moved by neither. Heavy, or light. Warm, or cool. Pleasant, or unpleasant. Let them arise, and pass... You remain, at the centre, aware...

<break time="12.0s" />

And when you are ready... repeat your resolve, once more...

<break time="5.0s" />

move gently... and let your eyes open.
